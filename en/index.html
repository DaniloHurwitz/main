      My approach doesn't start on the timeline

      Before I open Premiere, I think about the person who will watch the video. How long they'll give it before deciding whether to keep watching or scroll. What information they need first. At what moment they lose interest and why.

      I study Medicine at the National University of Mar del Plata. I don't say it to impress; I say it because it changed how I think about content. Studying how attention, perception and memory work, how the brain processes visual and auditory information, gave me a framework I apply every time I edit.

      I'm not a neuroscientist. But thinking analytically about people (how they process, what they retain, what they ignore) is something I do all day, at university and on the timeline.

      What I keep in mind when editing

      1. The first 2 seconds decide whether someone stays
      2. Cutting rhythm affects retention more than the content itself
      3. Visual changes prevent attention from fading
      4. Captions change how a message is understood
      5. Sound changes how energetic a video feels
      6. Visual hierarchy guides the eye without the viewer noticing
